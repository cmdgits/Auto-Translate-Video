PROMPT THIET KE VA XAY DUNG HE THONG AUTO DICH VIDEO DA NGON NGU SANG TIENG VIET

Muc dich cua file nay:
- Day la prompt/spec tong the de AI co the xay dung hoan chinh mot he thong tu dong xu ly video da tai san, nhan dien ngon ngu noi, chuyen thanh van ban, dich sang tieng Viet, tao phu de va tuy chon long tieng.
- Workspace hien tai dang trong, vi vay can thiet ke du an tu dau.
- Uu tien he thong chay tot tren Windows, co the mo rong sang Linux sau.

====================================================================
1. MUC TIEU SAN PHAM
====================================================================

Hay xay dung mot he thong "Auto Translate Video to Vietnamese" voi cac kha nang sau:

1. Nhan video dau vao tu thu muc local.
2. Tu dong phat hien ngon ngu noi trong video.
3. Tach audio tu video.
4. Chuyen giong noi thanh van ban co moc thoi gian.
5. Dich noi dung tu nhieu ngon ngu sang tieng Viet.
6. Tao file phu de tieng Viet dang SRT va VTT.
7. Tuy chon ghi phu de vao video hoac gan subtitle mem.
8. Tuy chon tao voice-over tieng Viet va ghep lai vao video.
9. Ho tro xu ly hang loat nhieu video.
10. Co log, co kha nang tiep tuc job dang do, co cau hinh ro rang.

Muc tieu uu tien:
- Chay duoc o may ca nhan.
- Uu tien chat luong va do on dinh hon toc do.
- Kien truc mo de sau nay co the doi backend ASR, backend dich, backend TTS.
- Co the dung offline-first, nhung cho phep tuy chon dung API neu nguoi dung muon chat luong dich cao hon.

====================================================================
2. DAU VAO, DAU RA, VA PHAM VI
====================================================================

Dau vao:
- Cac file video da tai san: mp4, mkv, mov, avi, webm.
- Co the co 1 video hoac nhieu video trong thu muc input.

Dau ra toi thieu:
- Ban transcript goc co timestamp.
- Ban dich tieng Viet co timestamp.
- File subtitle .srt
- File subtitle .vtt
- Video dau ra co subtitle mem hoac subtitle hardcode.

Dau ra nang cao:
- File audio voice-over tieng Viet.
- Video co long tieng tieng Viet.
- Bao cao JSON tong hop metadata, trang thai job, thoi gian xu ly, loi neu co.

Ngoai pham vi giai doan 1:
- Streaming realtime.
- Dubbing theo khau hinh lip-sync nang cao.
- Dashboard web phuc tap voi queue phan tan.

====================================================================
3. YEU CAU CHUC NANG CHI TIET
====================================================================

A. Quan ly input
- Cho phep chi dinh:
  - 1 file video cu the.
  - Ca mot thu muc input.
  - Che do quet thu muc va xu ly hang loat.
- Bo qua file da xu ly thanh cong neu nguoi dung bat che do resume.
- Dat ten thu muc output theo ten file video hoac theo ma job.

B. Phan tich media
- Doc metadata video bang ffprobe:
  - do dai video
  - codec
  - sample rate
  - so kenh audio
  - subtitle co san hay khong
- Neu video co nhieu track audio, cho phep chon track mac dinh hoac auto chon.

C. Tach audio va tien xu ly
- Dung ffmpeg de tach audio thanh wav mono 16k hoac 24k.
- Chuan hoa am luong neu can.
- Neu audio qua dai, cat thanh chunks nhung van giu duoc mapping timestamp chinh xac.
- Can nhac voice activity detection de bo khoang lang dai.

D. Speech-to-Text
- Backend mac dinh de xuat:
  - faster-whisper voi model medium hoac large-v3 neu may du tai nguyen.
- He thong phai:
  - auto detect language
  - tao transcript theo segment co start/end
  - co kha nang lua chon precision CPU/GPU
  - luu transcript goc ra JSON va text
- Neu gap video da ngon ngu hoac chat luong kem, can co option tinh chinh beam size, vad filter, chunk strategy.

E. Dich sang tieng Viet
- Dich tat ca segment transcript sang tieng Viet.
- Giu nguyen timestamp.
- Uu tien dich dung nghia, tu nhien, de doc, khong dich word-by-word.
- Giu nguyen:
  - ten rieng
  - ten thuong hieu
  - ma san pham
  - URL, email, so dien thoai
- Co co che glossary tu ngu tuy chinh.
- Co backend abstraction:
  - Local translation backend, vi du NLLB-200 hoac SeamlessM4T neu kha thi.
  - API translation backend, vi du OpenAI hoac Google Translate, neu nguoi dung cung cap key.

F. Hau xu ly subtitle
- Gom hoac tach segment de subtitle de doc:
  - moi dong khong qua dai
  - moi subtitle khong qua 2 dong neu co the
  - tranh nhap nhay qua nhanh
- Sinh file SRT va VTT chuan.
- Tuy chon tao ASS neu can style dep hon.

G. Tao video dau ra
- Mode 1: chi xuat file subtitle.
- Mode 2: mux subtitle mem vao video.
- Mode 3: burn subtitle truc tiep vao video.
- Dung ffmpeg de ghep file subtitle vao output.

H. Voice-over tieng Viet tuy chon
- Neu bat che do long tieng:
  - Tao TTS tieng Viet cho tung segment.
  - Can chinh toc do de khop tuong doi voi khoang thoi gian goc.
  - Mix voice moi voi nhac nen goc.
  - Ha volume kenh goc khi co loi thoai.
- Backend TTS de xuat:
  - edge-tts cho giai doan dau
  - co the nang cap XTTS, F5-TTS, hoac backend khac sau

I. Giao dien su dung
- Giai doan 1 bat buoc co CLI.
- Co the them giao dien web nhe bang Gradio hoac Streamlit sau khi pipeline on dinh.
- CLI can co cac lenh mau:
  - xu ly 1 file
  - xu ly ca thu muc
  - bat/tat subtitle hardcode
  - bat/tat voice-over
  - chon backend dich
  - chon model ASR

J. Quan ly loi va log
- Ghi log theo tung job.
- Neu 1 video loi thi khong lam dung ca lo.
- Luu file status JSON de resume.
- Co thong diep loi ro rang: thieu ffmpeg, thieu model, loi API key, loi audio corrupt.

7. LUONG XU LY CHUAN
====================================================================

Luong xu ly mong muon cho moi video:

1. Nhan file dau vao.
2. Tao job_id va thu muc output.
3. Doc metadata bang ffprobe.
4. Tach audio thanh wav chuan.
5. Chay ASR de lay transcript goc + language detection.
6. Hau xu ly segment transcript.
7. Dich tung segment sang tieng Viet.
8. Format subtitle de de doc.
9. Xuat transcript.json, translated.json, output.srt, output.vtt.
10. Neu bat subtitle mux:
    - tao video co subtitle mem hoac hard subtitle.
11. Neu bat voice-over:
    - tao audio TTS
    - can chinh duration
    - mix vao video
12. Ghi manifest.json ket qua job.
